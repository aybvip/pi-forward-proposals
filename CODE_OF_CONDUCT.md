# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be precise, be civil, and argue with evidence. Do not harass, impersonate, or
promote.

## Expected behaviour

- Critique the proposal, not the person.
- Cite the exact section, formula, or source line.
- State your uncertainty and your conflicts of interest.
- Accept that a proposal can be wrong, including your own.
- Respect privacy: no personal data, no doxxing, no KYC material.
- Respect the addressee: a draft is not official until the addressee says so.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Impersonation of Pi Network, the Pi Core Team, a PiRC maintainer, or another
  contributor.
- Posting private keys, seed phrases, wallet addresses, or personal data.
- Price pumping, referral spam, or investment solicitation.
- Repeatedly reopening a closed objection without new evidence.
- Deliberately misrepresenting a draft as accepted or official.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where someone is
representing this repository.

## Enforcement

A maintainer may edit, hide, or remove content, and may block an account for
repeated violations. Enforcement decisions are made in public when possible, and
privately when the content itself is sensitive.

There is no appeals committee. If you believe a decision was wrong, open a new issue
with the specific facts and a link to the decision. Do not harass the maintainer.
